About J&W Windows

A family business built on hard work, trust, and spotless results.

Our Story

Jon and Will Meunier each spent three years working for different window cleaning companies. As those businesses grew, the quality slipped, and the work became more about speed than care. They decided to start J&W Windows to keep the service personal, consistent, and focused on doing every job right.

At least one owner is on every single job to ensure the work matches the standard they promise. After over 30 jobs, they have found that J&W Windows is a more premium service than the typical company that hires random crews from job boards.

We treat every home like our own and stand behind every clean with the kind of attention to detail that comes from doing it ourselves.

Will Meunier

Co-founder. Will brings discipline and a sharp eye to every job. He makes sure every window is finished to the same high standard, no shortcuts.

Jon Meunier

Co-founder. Handles sales, client relationships, and on-site cleaning. Jon leads with energy and attention to detail that sets the standard for every job.

What Sets Us Apart

We're not a franchise. We're not a faceless crew. When you book with J&W Windows, you get us - the same people every time, who know your property and care about doing the job right.

We serve homeowners across Chester County, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and Carbon County, including Lake Harmony and Towamensing Trails. We always follow up to make sure you're satisfied.
