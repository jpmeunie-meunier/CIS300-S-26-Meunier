Our Story

Why we started
J&W Windows

Jon and Will Meunier each spent three years working for different window cleaning companies. As those businesses grew, the quality slipped and the work became more about speed than care.

They decided to start J&W Windows to keep the service personal, consistent, and focused on doing every job right. At least one owner is on every single job — that's not a marketing claim, it's how they run the company.

After over 30 jobs, they've found that J&W delivers a more premium experience than the typical company that hires random crews from job boards. They treat every home like their own.
